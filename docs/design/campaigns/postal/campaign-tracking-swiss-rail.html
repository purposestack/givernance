Campaign tracking — Swiss rail

Extension of the existing qr-tracking-card.tsx for postal campaigns. The same component renders three variants based on the campaign's postal_run_mode and Stripe-connect status.

Mode resolution (docs/25 §1.bis.0)

Variant chosen at render-time from getCampaignQrStats() shape:
– (a) Mixed — stripeRailActive=true AND bankAccountId set
– (b) Pure-Swiss — stripeRailActive=false AND bankAccountId set
– (c) Door-drop — mode=door_drop, scan stage null

Funnel anatomy (§5.3)

Three-stage core: printed → scanned → paid. The paid stage branches into the two payment rails (Stripe + Swiss). The "scanned but not paid" cohort is the remarketing target — always shown when scan tracking exists.

Door-drop omissions (§5.3 table)

Mode-aware fields return null on door-drop because the signal doesn't exist: no per-letter QR, no scan, no Stripe pre-fill. The component must visually omit those rows (not render them faded) — operator must not misread an unfilled metric as "0 scans".

API endpoints touched

GET /v1/campaigns/:id/qr-stats (no new endpoint — extends existing shape with paidQrBills, paidQrBillAmountCents, pendingQrBills, partialMatchCount, unreconciledCount, lastStatementImportedAt).

Partial-match badge (§5.3)

partialMatchCount only counted when the printed QR-bill carried a non-zero amount and the donor adjusted it. Donor-fills (printed amount = blank) never produce a "partial" — they're always full-match.

Unreconciled link

Click-through opens camt-unreconciled-queue.html filtered to campaign_id = $1. Closes the loop between "I see 3 pending" and "let me act on them".

Postal campaign tracking — three variants

Same component, three render paths chosen at request time from the campaign's mode + bank-account binding. The funnel structure is preserved across all three — only the rows that have no underlying signal are omitted.

(a) Mixed campaign · Stripe + Swiss QR-bill

The most common case for an NPO with both a Stripe-Connect account and a Swiss bank account on file. Donors choose their rail: scan + pay online, or detach the QR-bill slip and pay by bank transfer.

(c) Door-drop campaign · anonymous letters

Anonymous distribution (newspaper insert, door-to-door). No per-letter QR code, no scan signal, no per-recipient pre-fill. Conversion is measured purely at payment time, with distinct constituent counts resolved from RltdPties.Dbtr on the camt.053 side.
