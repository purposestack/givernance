# NPO Domain Glossary

This glossary defines standard terminology used in the non-profit sector, particularly for donor management and fundraising campaigns.

## Donor Segmentation Terms

### LYBUNT (Last Year But Unfortunately Not This)
- **Definition**: Donors who contributed in the previous calendar/fiscal year but have not given in the current year
- **Usage**: Key metric for identifying at-risk donors who may need re-engagement
- **Example**: "We have 234 LYBUNT donors who need attention before year-end"

### SYBUNT (Some Year But Unfortunately Not This)
- **Definition**: Donors who have given at some point in the past but not in the current year
- **Usage**: Broader category than LYBUNT, includes all lapsed donors
- **Example**: "Our SYBUNT report shows 1,200 donors haven't given this year"

### PYBUNT (Prior Year But Unfortunately Not This)
- **Definition**: Donors who gave two or more years ago but not recently
- **Usage**: Identifies long-lapsed donors for win-back campaigns
- **Example**: "PYBUNT donors often need different messaging than recent lapsers"

### Major Donor
- **Definition**: Donors who contribute above a certain threshold (varies by organization)
- **Common Thresholds**: €1,000+, €5,000+, or €10,000+ annually
- **Usage**: Segment requiring personalized cultivation and stewardship
- **Example**: "Major donors receive quarterly impact reports"

### Recurring Donor
- **Definition**: Donors who give on a regular schedule (monthly, quarterly, annually)
- **Usage**: Most valuable segment due to predictable revenue and higher lifetime value
- **Types**:
  - Monthly sustainers
  - Quarterly contributors
  - Annual members
- **Example**: "Our 450 monthly donors provide €225,000 in annual revenue"

### Lapsed Donor
- **Definition**: Previously active donors who haven't given within a defined period
- **Common Periods**: 12 months, 18 months, or 24 months
- **Usage**: Target for reactivation campaigns
- **Example**: "Send the lapsed donor campaign to anyone 13-24 months inactive"

### New Donor
- **Definition**: First-time donors within a recent period (typically 0-12 months)
- **Usage**: Critical segment for retention efforts
- **Example**: "New donors get a special welcome series for the first 90 days"

### Upgraded Donor
- **Definition**: Donors who increased their giving amount compared to previous gifts
- **Usage**: Indicates growing engagement and capacity
- **Example**: "123 donors upgraded their gifts by 25% or more this year"

## Engagement Metrics

### Donor Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **Definition**: Total amount donated over the entire donor relationship
- **Calculation**: Sum of all donations from first gift to present
- **Usage**: Identifies most valuable supporters for cultivation
- **Example**: "Focus major gift asks on donors with €5,000+ lifetime value"

### Average Gift Size
- **Definition**: Mean donation amount for a donor or segment
- **Calculation**: Total donated ÷ number of donations
- **Usage**: Helps set appropriate ask amounts
- **Example**: "Her average gift is €250, so ask for €500 as an upgrade"

### Giving Frequency
- **Definition**: How often a donor contributes (gifts per year)
- **Usage**: Identifies engagement level and upgrade potential
- **Example**: "Donors giving 3+ times annually are prime monthly giving prospects"

### Recency
- **Definition**: Time since last donation
- **RFM Context**: The "R" in RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis
- **Usage**: Key indicator of donor engagement and lapse risk
- **Example**: "Donors with recency > 18 months need reactivation"

### Retention Rate
- **Definition**: Percentage of donors who give again within a defined period
- **Calculation**: (Repeat donors ÷ total donors) × 100
- **Industry Benchmark**: ~45% for first-time donors, ~60% for repeat donors
- **Example**: "Our 68% retention rate exceeds sector benchmarks"

## Campaign Types

### Annual Appeal
- **Definition**: Yearly comprehensive fundraising campaign
- **Timing**: Often year-end (November-December) or fiscal year-end
- **Usage**: Core unrestricted revenue generator
- **Example**: "The annual appeal raised €450,000 from 1,200 donors"

### Major Gift Campaign
- **Definition**: Targeted solicitation of high-value donations
- **Typical Ask**: €1,000+ (varies by organization size)
- **Usage**: Personal cultivation for transformational gifts
- **Example**: "The major gift campaign seeks 50 gifts of €10,000+"

### Acquisition Campaign
- **Definition**: Efforts focused on attracting new donors
- **Metrics**: Cost per acquisition, first-gift size
- **Usage**: Expand donor base
- **Example**: "Our acquisition campaign brought in 300 new donors"

### Retention Campaign
- **Definition**: Targeted efforts to keep existing donors engaged
- **Focus**: Second gifts, lapsed reactivation
- **Usage**: More cost-effective than acquisition
- **Example**: "The retention campaign achieved 72% second-gift conversion"

### Reactivation Campaign
- **Definition**: Win back lapsed donors
- **Target**: LYBUNT and SYBUNT segments
- **Usage**: Re-engage previously committed supporters
- **Example**: "Reactivation emails brought back 15% of lapsed donors"

## Swiss-Specific Terms

### Canton
- **Definition**: Swiss federal state (26 total)
- **Usage**: Geographic segmentation for campaigns
- **Example**: "Focus on Geneva and Vaud cantons for this appeal"

### Commune
- **Definition**: Swiss municipality
- **Usage**: Local targeting for community campaigns
- **Example**: "All Lausanne commune residents get the local impact report"

### Association (1901 Law)
- **Definition**: French non-profit legal structure common in Romandy
- **Usage**: Most common NPO structure in French-speaking Switzerland
- **Example**: "As a 1901 association, we can receive tax-deductible gifts"
